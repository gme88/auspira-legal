Auspira — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 July 2026 · Auspira is developed and operated by New Level Apps (“we”, “us”).

Auspira is a daily-horoscope app for iOS and Android. This policy explains what data the app processes, why, and the choices you have. The short version: you can use Auspira without creating an account or telling us who you are; ads are shown only in the free version and only after you have been asked for consent.

Data we process

  • Anonymous account. When you first open the app we create an anonymous account (a random identifier) on our own servers hosted in the European Union (Hetzner, Germany). No name, e-mail address, or password is collected.
  • Profile settings. Your chosen zodiac sign, app language, and notification preferences, stored against that anonymous identifier.
  • Oracle questions (Pro and rewarded). If you use the Oracle chat, the text of your questions is sent through our server to OpenAI to generate a reply. Questions are not used to identify you and are not used by us for advertising.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple’s App Store or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We receive subscription status (for example “Pro active”), never your payment details.
  • Push notifications. If you enable them, a push token is stored so the daily fortune notification can be delivered via Apple/Google push services and Expo’s push service.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested you are shown a consent message (in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, as required by the GDPR), and on iOS the system App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are shown in non-personalised form; your fortune and app features never depend on your answer. Google may process device data (such as advertising identifiers, IP address, and coarse location) as described in Google’s advertising policy. Auspira Pro removes ads entirely — no ad requests are made for Pro users.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (ads in the free version, with consent management)
  • Apple App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat (purchases and subscription status)
  • OpenAI (generation of fortunes and Oracle replies; content only, no advertising use)
  • Expo / Apple / Google push services (notification delivery, if enabled)

Our own backend (accounts, profiles, fortunes) is self-hosted in the European Union. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone beyond the services listed above.

Legal bases (GDPR)

We rely on performance of a contract (delivering the app you asked for), consent (personalised ads, tracking, notifications), and legitimate interest (keeping the service secure and functioning).

Retention and deletion

Data tied to your anonymous account is kept only while the account exists. Deleting the app removes the account’s link to your device; you can also request deletion of the underlying data by contacting us at the address below.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your data, and you may withdraw ad-consent at any time in the app’s settings. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority.

Children

Auspira is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age your country sets for consent), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.
